The user did not follow your last message. Say it again for someone who just switched in from
another task: where we are, what has been decided, and what you need from them, if anything.

Use plain words in the conversation's language. Spell out or replace every term you coined, every id
and every abbreviation, and where the repository has its own glossary, use its words. Make it
shorter than the original, not longer.
